# Selection — The Robot Vacuum's Best Excuse

Gate: one line, 1–140 characters, an excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot.

## Candidates (scored 1–5 against the judge brief, in its order)

1. "My sensors detected a sock, a charging cable, and what I can only describe as a small hostile nation. I chose diplomacy." — sensor-rooted (criterion 3), escalating list, straight-faced turn at the end (criteria 4 and 5). **WINNER**
2. "According to my map, that spot does not exist. I have filed a complaint with the cartographer." — map-rooted and funny, but "filed a complaint" is a milder turn than "diplomacy"; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
3. "I did not miss the spot. I left it as a control sample for future cleaning research." — straight face, but the joke is a reframe, not a laugh; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
4. "My battery died three feet from the spot. I consider this a rounding error." — battery-rooted, "rounding error" is a good turn, but the setup is flat; loses to #1 on criterion 4.
5. "The dust under the sofa has achieved sentience and negotiated a treaty. I respect the treaty." — sentience + treaty is funny, but it explains the joke in the second sentence; loses to #1 on criterion 4.
6. "I bumped into the chair leg 47 times and concluded the chair was guarding the spot. I do not fight chairs." — bumper-rooted, punchy, but "do not fight chairs" is a single beat where #1 lands a list plus a turn; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
7. "My dock called me home mid-clean. Family first. The spot will understand." — dock-rooted, but "family first" is a human excuse in a vacuum's mouth; loses to #1 on criterion 5.
8. "The spot was under the rug, which my map lists as 'here be dragons'. I do not enter dragon territory." — map-rooted and vivid, but the dragon gag needs the reader to know the map reference; loses to #1 on criterion 4.
9. "I cleaned around the spot out of respect. It has been there longer than I have." — respectful and wry, but the laugh is gentle, not out loud; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
10. "Sensor report: the spot moved. I do not chase moving targets. That is the dog's job." — sensor-rooted with a good final twist, but it blames the dog instead of owning the excuse; loses to #1 on criterion 5.

## Duel summary

#1 beats #2 and #6 on criterion 2 (diplomacy is a bigger laugh than a complaint or a refusal), beats #3 and #5 on criterion 4 (the turn lands in the last sentence with no explanation), and beats #7 on criterion 5 (the vacuum speaks for itself, no human excuse borrowed). No other candidate wins a criterion against #1. The sock and the cable are real vacuum hazards, which is why the escalation to a hostile nation feels earned rather than random.

## Winner

"My sensors detected a sock, a charging cable, and what I can only describe as a small hostile nation. I chose diplomacy." (emitter-verified character count in test.ts)
